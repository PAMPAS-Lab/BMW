The target is elapsed time from receiving a clear topic to delivering the current, independently reviewed MP4 and its written script. Source acquisition, identity/date checks, source speech and Chinese translation, editing, rendering, whole-film review and any repair are included. A successful acquisition or renderer test alone cannot prove the target.

## Implemented optimization under verification

- `library.sources acquire method:auto`: preserve at most two observed deliveries from the confirmed finite target, sharing a 60-second download budget. Successful preservation retains encoded tracks and proof; no capture follows success.
- On failure, native workers, network and files must drain before fallback. Cancellation, changed target/Project and failed cleanup cannot release capture admission. Explicit long archives retain their separate budget.
- Capture fallback requests a supplied YouTube player quality up to 1080p and records the requested setting separately from observed dimensions. Actual file dimensions and audio tracks require decode verification. No claim of an upload master or lossless capture.
- Navigation can finish once the actual matching YouTube player has decoded video. Merely receiving a page or thumbnail cannot establish readiness. Cold initialization has a finite ceiling; stopping page loading too early can prevent the player from ever appearing.
- `video.edit clip.crop` targets the real primary scene/segment without roundtripping draft summaries or changing audio/subtitle clocks. This addresses repeated failed reframing and avoids exporting a known-bad composition.
- Per-input Codex developer scope supersedes historical audit-only text after audit cancellation/completion has drained; the active audit guard remains enforced. This avoids repeated refused edits and preserves the Session resume anchor.
